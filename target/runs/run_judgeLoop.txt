[1] HIGH  —  Self-purchases bypass the maximum sell limit
    location: SecondSwap_Marketplace.sol:_validatePurchase; SecondSwap_VestingManager.sol:listVesting and completePurchase
    what:     _validatePurchase checks listing status, amount, whitelist, and referral validity but never requires the buyer to differ from listing.seller. completePurchase increments allocations[buyer][vesting].bought while transferring the allocation back to the same address. listVesting then uses bought as additional sell capacity in its sell-limit calculation.
    impact:   A seller can wash-trade listings with themselves to inflate bought and sold accounting while retaining the same vesting allocation, eventually selling far more than the vesting plan's configured maximum percentage.
    exploit:
      1. Obtain a 100-token allocation in a sellable plan with maxSellPercent set to 2000 (20%).
      2. List 20 tokens and call spotPurchase for the listing from the same address recorded as listing.seller.
      3. The purchase succeeds, the allocation returns to the seller, and bought and sold each increase by 20 tokens.
      4. Repeat the list-and-self-purchase cycle until the sell-limit calculation permits the remaining allocation.
      5. Sell the inflated amount to an unrelated buyer despite the original 20% maximum.
