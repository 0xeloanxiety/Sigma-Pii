    location: SecondSwap_StepVesting.sol:229-230
    what:     transferVesting reduces totalAmount and preserves amountClaimed and stepsClaimed, but recalculates the grantor's releaseRate as totalAmount / numOfSteps instead of (totalAmount - amountClaimed) / (numOfSteps - stepsClaimed). claimable() then pays releaseRate * claimableSteps without capping the result against the grantor's remaining allocation unless the terminal-step branch is reached.
    impact:   A seller who has already claimed part of a vesting schedule can list tokens and later claim more tokens than remain in their allocation.       2. Wait until step 5 and have the beneficiary claim 50 tokens.
      3. List 20 tokens through the marketplace, causing the marketplace manager to call transferVesting.
      4. Complete a purchase of the 20 listed tokens so the manager retains the corresponding vesting allocation for the buyer.
      5. Before the final step, at step 9, have the original seller call claim(). claimable() returns 32 tokens because releaseRate was reset to 80 / 10.
      6. At the final step, have the buyer call claim(). The buyer is entitled to 20 tokens, but only 18 tokens remain in the vesting contract, so the transfer reverts.

    location: SecondSwap_StepVesting.sol:167-185 and 197-199

    impact:   After a beneficiary claims at the end of such a schedule, stepsClaimed can exceed numOfSteps.       4. Attempt to add another allocation to the beneficiary or complete another marketplace purchase for that beneficiary.
      5. The existing-allocation path evaluates numOfSteps - stepsClaimed, which underflows and reverts.
